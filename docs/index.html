How to play

  • 📷 Prop the phone up, angled at your floor. Use the back camera if you can.
  • 🟧 Calibrate: tap the 4 corners of your real play area so the game knows your floor.
  • 🧍 You appear inside a dungeon, standing on brick tiles.
  • ⏱️ Each round you get a few seconds to pick a tile.
  • 💥 Then the stage rumbles and the unsafe tiles crumble into the lava.
  • ✅ Land on solid stone to survive. Last as long as you can!
